## Hand-off note: self-signed-certificate client authenticator

### What to imitate
`services/.../authentication/authenticators/client/X509ClientAuthenticator.java` (PROVIDER_ID `client-x509`) is the model. Copy its front half verbatim in spirit: get `X509ClientCertificateLookup` from the session, resolve `client_id` from form data → query params → session attribute, `ClientAuthUtil.errorResponse` for a missing id, `CLIENT_NOT_FOUND` / `CLIENT_DISABLED` failures, `context.setClient`, and `context.attempted()` on `GeneralSecurityException` / absent certs. Replace everything after that: instead of chain validation (`CertificateValidator`) and subject-DN / CA-subject-DN matching, compare the leaf certificate presented in the handshake byte-for-byte against the certificate stored on the client. Do **not** consult a trust store.

The stored certificate lives in client attributes: `CertificateInfoHelper.getCertificateFromClient(client, prefix)` returns a `CertificateRepresentation` whose `getCertificate()` is the PEM (attribute `<prefix>.certificate`). Parse with `PemUtils.decodeCertificate` (used widely, e.g. `ClientAttributeCertificateResource`). Byte-for-byte means the encoded form, not subject+key — a re-issued cert with identical DN and key must fail.

Two recent authenticators show the full wiring of a *new* provider: `FederatedJWTClientAuthenticator` (commit 57242d2) and `AttestationBasedClientAuthenticator` (commit 22e018c). Both touched: the authenticator class, `services/src/main/resources/META-INF/services/org.keycloak.authentication.ClientAuthenticatorFactory`, `DefaultAuthenticationFlows.clientAuthenticationFlow` (ALTERNATIVE execution, unique priority; existing ones are 5/10/20/30/40/50), and `OIDCWellKnownProvider`.

### Dependency order
1. New class extending `AbstractClientAuthenticator`; pick a `PROVIDER_ID` and a new OIDC auth-method constant in `services/.../protocol/oidc/OIDCLoginProtocol.java` (next to `TLS_CLIENT_AUTH`, line 129).
2. Return that method from `getProtocolAuthenticatorMethods(OIDCLoginProtocol.LOGIN_PROTOCOL)` — that alone feeds `OIDCWellKnownProvider.getClientAuthMethodsSupported()` (token/introspection/revocation lists) and `AuthorizeClientUtil.findClientAuthenticatorForOIDCAuthMethod` / `DescriptionConverter`, i.e. dynamic registration by name.
3. Register in the service file, and add an execution in `DefaultAuthenticationFlows`.
4. Add the provider id to every `allowed-client-authenticators` list in `services/src/main/resources/keycloak-default-client-profiles.json` (7 occurrences alongside `client-x509`).
